Night once more falls over the City, and the two leave the shop. Despite Jacks 
offers Sonja is low on charge, so she saves her words and leaves the navigation 
to her companion. \
Besides a day's sleep and words of encouragement, Jack also gave them a token
to present to the people they're gonna meet, to show them they aren't enemies. 
After all Sonja still has her companies' markings, the marks of machines that 
used to oppress the people in these streets. \
Their journey takes them deeper into the city, the houses around them become
more decorated and elaborate with every passing hour. Still in shambles in many
places, but you can tell the people that used to live there were much richer --
only that their money is useless now. \
After crossing a smaller plaza that leads into a bigger one, they spot a large
building that used to belong to military. The doors have fortifications 
constructed from wood and scavenged metal, with guns hoisted onto them. 
According to Jacks information this is a central outpost for the gang controlling
these streets, and the boss they are looking for is currently residing there.

Carefully and with slow steps the two approach the large building, Sonja half
hidden behind Shiv, who herself carries the token she was given in her hand. 
They come within fifty steps of the front door before finally someone peeks out
from behind the barricade: "What do you want?" \
Without pause Shiv raises the token high into the air, and moderately raises 
her voice: "We're friends. Looking to offer our services". \
The person that just now interrogated them gives a quick shout to the side the 
two can't hear, and a flood light from a first floor window suddenly blinds 
them. \
Instinctively Sonja raises her hands slightly to show she's not armed, and Shiv
remains in place with the token raised high. As they adjust to the sudden 
brightness they see the person from before inspect them with binoculars. They
remain frozen for agonizing seconds as they wait for further instructions. \
After another quick shout, the light turns off again, and darkness and moonlight 
return to the plaza. The guard finally gives them the go: "Okay, come inside". \
Cautiously they make their way to the barricade, which shortly gets pushed
to the side just enough to let them through one after another. First Shiv gets
to step through, with the token grasped to her chest just in case. With a moment of 
hesitation Sonja follows. \
The barricade gets shoved back into place behind them. They adjust to the 
new light and find themselves surrounded by half a dozen armed people. They 
eye them carefully, but are especially weary of Sonja. \
One of them steps towards Shiv, and greets them: "So Jack sent you?", he puts
out his hand expectantly. \
Shiv puts the token she carries into the mans hand, and puts on as non-threatening
a smile she can manage: "Yeah, we heard you run things 'round here". \
"As much as you can run this place", the man jokes, putting the token into one of 
his many pockets. "The thing behind you... you want Jack to work on it? Is it
safe?" \
Not exactly spoken to, Sonja steps outside of Shivs shadow: "All safe and 
friendly, just looking to get some work done". \
The other five are momentarily startled, but the man securing the hallway behind
him doesn't flinch: "Friendly's good. We're quite busy, so there's always work
to go around. Hope you're ready to get you're hands dirty". \
To avoid Sonja further agitating the guards, Shiv tries to quickly wrap up
the smalltalk: "Got dirty hands already. Please show us to your boss, and we
take some load off your shoulders". \
The soldier eyes Shiv carefully once more, then eyes Sonja, then Shiv again. 
But a moment later he lets his shoulders relax: "Guess we'll see about that. 
Follow me".

They are lead down two floors, into what feels like a bunker. The walls are
naked concrete and the lighting is harsh and bright. Every doorframe they pass 
they see at least one armed person keeping watch, and only a few civilian 
looking people walk about or reside in rooms they pass. \
At the end of this brutalist hallway they enter a larger room, with tables all 
around full of documents, and one in the center of the room full of maps. At 
that table stands an older woman facing the door, but with her gaze fixated on
a document before her. Sonja guesses her age to be around 40 to 50, her very 
short hair is a mixture of brown and white, and her face looks sleep deprived. 
They stop just behind the door, when the man escorting them stops. He
steps to the side and addresses the woman at the table: "Nora, got visitors. 
Jack sent 'em". \
After finishing whatever paragraph she was reading, the mans boss lifts her
eyes off the table and towards the two unfamiliar faces. Her gaze lingers on
Shiv, then on Sonja, who both grow nervous being met with this much silence. She

now alluring more to a uniform: "What do you have to offer?" \
Eager to get on with it, Sonja answers: "My companion is a capable mechanic, 
I'm a military grade android from Fenrir. What would it cost to have Jack work 
on me?" \
Shiv wanted to speak as well, but seeing how her friend took the initiative 
she takes a back seat for now. \
Nora takes a few steps around the table, never taking her eyes off the
two. She stops in front of the table, five steps short of her visitors: "A
Fenrir doll? Can you fight?" \
"With Jacks mods I could", Sonja quickly responds. \
The old woman gives her a smirk: "I see". Her eyes wander over to Shiv, being
the next target of her interrogation: "Mechanic?" \
Mimicking Sonja, she gives a quick response as well: "Worked on everything from
trucks to Fenrir mechs, in fancy shops and on scrappy streets. I can make 
almost anything work". \
Once more the woman smiles: "Good". Her posture remains stiff, but the tone of
her voice softens slightly: "I'm Nora, I run things around here". \
Both Shiv and Sonja shortly give their names, then obediently wait for the 
conversation to be continued. \
With little hesitation Nora gets back to business: "The work Jack does is very
important to us. They are the only one that can maintain much of the enhancements

a lot of trust. So you better be ready to work for it". \
It takes a lot of restraint in Sonja to not be frustrated once more, having to

everyone around her has. \
Sensing her tension, Shiv jumps in: "Getting here was expensive and dangerous, 
we're not gonna stop before we've gotten my girl here her repairs". \
For a moment, the tension in Shivs companion passes, overshadowed by the 
surprisingly affectionate words. Metal can't blush, but her voice is slightly
timid: "Shiv paid a lot getting me here, can't disappoint her now can I". \
Entertained Nora's mood brightens: "Good. I've got quite a lot of lonely wolves
pass through my camp, so seeing people that understand companionship here is
quite refreshing". \
Shiv gives a polite chuckle: "Ain't another way you survive these streets is 
there?" \
"I fear so", Nora agrees. "But enough of that, let's find you some work".
